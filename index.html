Broker Credit Doctor

A zero-dependency Python CLI that audits a carrier's broker payment history CSV export for the payment risks that cost small carriers real money: invoices overdue past their terms, invoices unpaid more than 90 days past due (surety bond claim territory), brokers that pay late again and again, one broker carrying too much of the book, the same broker name under two different MC numbers (a double brokering signal), missing or invalid MC numbers, duplicate invoice ids, malformed rows, and unreadable dates. It also prints the average days to pay, the on-time payment rate, and a per-broker summary table, so a dispatcher can see which brokers to keep booking and which to avoid.
